Products & Services

One account, a complete record of your financial activity with the cooperative.

Savings & contributions

Regular contributions are the foundation of membership. You commit to an affordable monthly amount, paid by salary deduction or directly, and every entry is recorded to your account.

  • Choose a contribution band that suits your income.
  • Pay by payroll deduction or direct transfer.
  • Contribute consistently; consistency builds eligibility.

Access to loans

Six loan products sized against your savings, with rates from 5% and tenures from 4 to 36 months — and every figure disclosed before you commit.

  • Limits from 3× up to 5× your savings.
  • Fixed monthly repayments and a clear schedule.
  • Full rates on the Loan Products & Rates page.

Digital member records

Your profile, membership details, contributions and loan history held digitally — retrievable and consistent, rather than reconstructed from a paper ledger.

Financial statements

Statements that stand up to scrutiny — for your own planning, or when a bank, landlord or employer asks for evidence of your savings and repayment behaviour.

Transparent transaction history

Every contribution, repayment, fee and adjustment is traceable to a date, an amount and a reference. If you cannot see it, you can ask about it — and get an answer.

Investment opportunities

Where applicable, members may participate in cooperative investment pools. Participation is voluntary, subject to scheme rules and approval, and is not a guaranteed return.

What is not included

Being clear about the boundaries

A financial platform earns trust by stating what it does not do. Coopvest Africa is a cooperative savings and financing platform. It is not a bank, and this website is not the place you manage your money.

  • Not a bank account. Contributions are cooperative savings, not a demand deposit.
  • Not an investment guarantee. Investment pools, where offered, carry risk.
  • Not this website. Balance and transaction management happens in the member app.
  • Not financial advice. We explain our products; we do not tell you what to do with your money.
