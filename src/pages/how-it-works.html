How It Works

From first contribution to a cleared loan balance — the whole process, step by step.

Getting started

Who can join

Coopvest serves two kinds of member. Both end up with the same benefits — the only difference is how contributions are paid.

Salary Deduction

Employees of partner institutions

If your employer partners with Coopvest Africa, you are onboarded during a payroll cycle and your contribution is deducted from salary each month.

  • Onboarded as part of an employer scheme.
  • Contribution authorised as a payroll deduction.
  • Deduction remitted and recorded automatically.
Direct Deposit

Direct members

No partner employer? You can still join and contribute directly, on the same platform and with the same records and protections.

  • Register and complete verification directly — no employment details needed.
  • Contribute by bank transfer, card or USSD, at your own pace.
  • Build the same savings history and loan eligibility.
Onboarding

The path into the cooperative

Employer / institution onboarding

We agree the scheme with your institution: who is eligible, the contribution bands, and the payroll cycle the deduction runs on. Your employer authorises the deduction structure once, for all participating staff.

Direct deposit onboarding

Direct members register with a verified email and phone number, and are assigned to the direct contribution scheme rather than a payroll scheme.

Registration & KYC

You complete a short identity verification step — the details that tie your account and records to a real person. This is a prerequisite for financial activity, including any future loan.

Monthly contributions

Your agreed amount is deducted from salary or paid directly each month. It is recorded against your account immediately, with a date, amount and reference you can review.

Loan eligibility

As your contribution history builds, so does your eligibility. Limits reflect what you have saved and your repayment capacity, and are confirmed at the point of application.

Loan repayment

Approved loans come with a fixed monthly repayment and a clear schedule. Repayments continue alongside your contributions, and the outstanding balance is visible at all times.

Member account management

Your contributions, loan status, statements and transaction history live in your member account on the Coopvest mobile app — separate from this website, which is for information only.
