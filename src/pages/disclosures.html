Risk & Financial Disclosures

Please read this before joining, contributing, or taking a loan.

Draft for legal review — last updated 2026. Regulatory and licensing details remain to be added once finalised.

Read this first

This page summarises the principal risks of participating in a cooperative savings and financing arrangement. It is not exhaustive and it is not financial advice. If you do not understand these risks, seek independent advice before joining.

1. Coopvest is not a bank

Coopvest Africa is a cooperative savings and financing platform. It is not a licensed deposit-taking bank, and contributions are not bank deposits. Cooperative savings do not carry the same protections, guarantees or compensation arrangements that may apply to deposits held with a licensed bank.

2. Your savings carry risk

Contributions are pooled and used to finance lending to other members and to meet the cooperative's obligations. If borrowers default at a scale the cooperative cannot absorb, the value available to members may be affected. Past performance is not a guide to future outcomes.

3. Lending and credit risk

Where you take a loan, you are obliged to repay it in full, including interest and any applicable charges, according to the agreement you accept. Failure to repay may result in penalties, recovery action, and loss of access to future financing.

4. Guarantor risk

If you guarantee another member's loan, you may be required to repay the outstanding balance if that member defaults. Do not guarantee a loan you are not prepared and able to repay yourself.

5. Investment pools, where offered

Any investment pool offered is voluntary and is not a guaranteed return. The value of a pool may fall as well as rise, and you may not recover the full amount you put in. Pool participation is subject to scheme rules and approval, and may not be available to every member.

6. Dependence on your employer

Where you participate through a salary deduction scheme, contributions depend on your employer continuing to deduct and remit, and on your continued employment. If your employment ends, deduction stops; your membership and records continue, and you may need to switch to direct contributions.

7. No guaranteed credit access

Building a contribution history improves your eligibility, but it does not create an entitlement to credit. Every application is assessed, and a loan may be declined or offered on terms different from those you expected.

8. Operational and technology risk

Like any digital platform, our services may be affected by outages, technical failure or security incidents. We work to prevent and mitigate these, but we cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability.

9. Charges and penalties

Fees, charges and penalties apply as disclosed in your schedule of charges and loan agreement. Late repayment may also affect your eligibility for future financing.

10. Regulatory status

Regulatory and licensing disclosures are being finalised and will be published on this page and in our Terms of Service once complete. Until then, this page should not be read as a statement of regulatory authorisation.

11. Contact
