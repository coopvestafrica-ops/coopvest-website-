How this page stays accurate

The figures above are generated from the cooperative's own fee data and published to this page automatically. They are not retyped by hand, because a figure written down twice eventually disagrees with itself — and a price list that disagrees with the price charged is worse than no price list at all.

Your own obligations are always visible in the member app, under Settings → About → Fees & Charges, together with any amount outstanding on your account.
