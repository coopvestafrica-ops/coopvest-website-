The problem we solve

Saving is hard when nothing is structured.

For most salaried workers, income arrives and quietly disappears. Emergency borrowing is expensive, informal savings are untracked, and access to fair credit is reserved for people who already have it.

Coopvest Africa replaces that uncertainty with a structured, cooperative model: a fixed, affordable contribution, a visible record of every naira, and a fair route to financing based on what you have actually saved.

  • Contribute an amount you choose, on a schedule that matches your salary.
  • Build eligibility for financing as your savings grow.
  • See your contributions, statements and balances in one place.

Trust & security

How we protect your money and your data

  • Identity before access. No account is activated for financial activity until identity verification is complete.
  • Encryption in transit. All traffic to our platform and APIs uses HTTPS with strict transport security.
  • Least-privilege operations. Internal staff can only reach the functions their role permits, and every action is logged.
  • Auditable money movement. Contributions, disbursements and repayments are recorded with a reference and a timestamp.

Questions

Answers to the things people ask first

Who can join Coopvest Africa?

Salaried workers whose employer partners with us, and individuals who register directly. Employees of partner institutions are onboarded during a payroll cycle; direct members contribute themselves.

How much can I contribute?

You contribute an amount you choose within the bands agreed for your scheme. The goal is consistency — an affordable amount you can maintain every month beats an ambitious one you stop paying.

When can I access a loan?

After you have completed identity verification and built a contribution history. Loan limits are based on your savings and repayment capacity, and are confirmed at application. See Loans for the detail.

How does salary deduction work?

You authorise your employer to deduct your contribution from salary each cycle. The employer remits the deduction and your member record updates automatically. You can review every deduction in your account.

What happens if I leave my employer?

Salary deduction stops, but your membership and records do not disappear. You can continue as a direct member and keep contributing. If you wish to exit, your position is settled under the scheme rules in force at the time.
