Loans

Affordable financing, earned through consistent saving and repaid on a schedule you can see.

Eligibility

Who can apply

Loans are available to members who have completed identity verification and built a contribution history with the cooperative. Eligibility is assessed on your savings behaviour, your repayment capacity and your standing as a member.

  • Active, verified membership.
  • A contribution history of the minimum duration set by your scheme.
  • No unresolved default or outstanding arrears.
  • Repayment capacity consistent with your deduction or income.

Savings-based loan limits

Loan limits are derived from what you have actually saved, not from an arbitrary ceiling. The stronger your contribution record, the more you can access — up to the multiple set for your scheme.

Illustrative only

The multiple of savings available, the interest rate and the maximum tenure vary by scheme and are confirmed in your loan offer. Nothing on this page is a quote or a commitment to lend.

Loan categories & tenures

Different needs, different terms

Categories exist so that a short-term need is not priced like a long-term one. The products and tenures available to you depend on your scheme.

Short tenure

Emergency & short-term

For immediate, time-bound needs. Repaid over a short period, with the smallest total interest cost.

  • Lower amounts
  • Brief repayment period
  • Fastest route to resolution
Medium tenure

Personal & planned expenses

For school fees, medical costs, rent or similar planned outlays, repaid over a moderate period.

  • Moderate amounts
  • Monthly instalments
  • Budgeted against salary
Longer tenure

Larger, longer-term needs

For substantial requirements where a longer repayment horizon keeps each instalment affordable.

  • Higher amounts, subject to limit
  • Extended repayment period
  • Higher total interest
Repayment process

How repayment works

Apply and receive an offer

You apply for an amount and tenure. You receive an offer stating the amount, the interest rate, the monthly repayment and the total repayment — before you accept.

Approval and disbursement

Once approved, funds are disbursed to your designated account and the loan becomes active with a first repayment date.

Fixed monthly repayment

Repayment is a fixed monthly instalment, aligned to your salary cycle where you are on a payroll scheme. Each instalment reduces the outstanding balance.

Tracking to zero

Your outstanding balance, repayment history and completion date are visible in your member account throughout the life of the loan.

Late repayment

Missed or late repayments may attract a penalty and affect your eligibility for future financing. Penalty amounts are stated in your loan agreement and in the schedule of charges provided at onboarding. If you are struggling to repay, contact us before you miss a payment — there is usually more we can do then than afterwards.

Security for a loan

Guarantor requirements

Where a scheme requires a guarantor, it exists to protect the members' pooled savings — not to create obstacles.

  • When a guarantor is required. Some schemes or loan categories require one, particularly for larger amounts or first loans.
  • Who can guarantee. Normally an active member in good standing, or as otherwise set out in scheme rules.
  • What they are committing to. The guarantor accepts responsibility for the outstanding balance if you default. This is a serious commitment and should be discussed openly.
  • How it is recorded. The guarantee is documented in your loan agreement and visible to the guarantor.

Before you guarantee someone

Guaranteeing a loan means you may be asked to repay it. Consider whether you can afford that outcome, and whether you would be comfortable doing so.
