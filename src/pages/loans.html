Eligibility

Who can apply

Loans are available to members who have completed identity verification and built a contribution history with the cooperative. Eligibility is assessed on your savings behaviour, your repayment capacity and your standing as a member.

  • Active, verified membership (KYC complete).
  • A savings balance to support the amount you are requesting.
  • Three guarantors able to accept your request.
  • No loan overdue, defaulted or in recovery — any of those blocks a new application.
  • Repayment capacity consistent with your deduction or income.

Savings-based loan limits

Loan limits are derived from what you have actually saved, not from an arbitrary ceiling. Your maximum is a multiple of your savings balance:

  • 3× your savings — Quick, Flexi and both Stable Loans.
  • 4× your savings — Premium Loan.
  • 5× your savings — Maxi Loan.

The minimum you can borrow is ₦1,000. So a member with ₦50,000 saved can borrow up to ₦150,000 on a Stable Loan, rising to ₦250,000 on a Maxi Loan.

Your limit is confirmed at application

The multiple above sets your ceiling. The amount you are offered also depends on your repayment capacity, and is stated in your loan offer before you accept. Full rates are on the Loan Products & Rates page.

Repayment process

How repayment works

Apply and receive an offer

You apply for an amount and tenure. You receive an offer stating the amount, the interest rate, the monthly repayment and the total repayment — before you accept.

Approval and disbursement

Once approved, funds are disbursed to your designated account and the loan becomes active with a first repayment date.

Fixed monthly repayment

Repayment is a fixed monthly instalment, aligned to your salary cycle where you are on a payroll scheme. Each instalment reduces the outstanding balance.

Tracking to zero

Your outstanding balance, repayment history and completion date are visible in your member account throughout the life of the loan.

Late repayment

Missed or late repayments may attract a penalty and affect your eligibility for future financing. Penalty amounts are stated in your loan agreement and in the schedule of charges provided at onboarding. If you are struggling to repay, contact us before you miss a payment — there is usually more we can do then than afterwards.

Security for a loan

Guarantor requirements

Every loan needs three guarantors. It exists to protect the members' pooled savings — not to create obstacles.

  • Three guarantors are required. Every Coopvest loan needs 3 guarantors before it can be approved.
  • How they are collected. You generate a QR code in the member app and each guarantor accepts it in turn. Approval clears once all three have.
  • Who can guarantee. Guarantors must be Coopvest members in good standing, as set out in scheme rules.
  • What they are committing to. A guarantor accepts responsibility for the outstanding balance if you default. This is a serious commitment and should be discussed openly.
  • How it is recorded. The guarantee is documented against your loan and visible to the guarantor.

Before you guarantee someone

Guaranteeing a loan means you may be asked to repay it. Consider whether you can afford that outcome, and whether you would be comfortable doing so.
