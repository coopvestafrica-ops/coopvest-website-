Cookie Policy

How this website uses cookies and local storage.

Draft for legal review — last updated 2026.

This policy explains how Coopvest Africa uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Local storage is a similar browser mechanism for storing small amounts of data. Both are used to remember information between page loads or visits.

2. What this website uses

This corporate website deliberately keeps its use of cookies and storage to a minimum. At present:

Storage used by this website
TypePurposeNecessary?
Local storageRetains layout preferences you set, such as collapsed navigation groups and the dismissed notice bannerOptional, but the site works without it
Font deliveryGoogle Fonts may set a cookie or log a request when the typeface is loadedNecessary for the intended appearance

3. What we do not use on this website

  • Advertising or retargeting cookies.
  • Third-party analytics trackers that profile you across other websites.
  • Session or authentication cookies — sign-in happens in the member app, not here.

4. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies and clear local storage through your browser settings. Doing so may affect how parts of this website display, but it will not prevent you from reading it or contacting us.

5. The member app is separate

When you sign in to the Coopvest mobile app, app-specific session and security data is stored on your device to keep you signed in and to protect your account. That is governed by our Privacy Policy rather than this policy.

6. Changes

If we introduce additional cookies or storage on this website — for example, privacy-respecting analytics — we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@coopvest.africa.
