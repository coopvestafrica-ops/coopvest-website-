Schedule of Charges

Every charge we apply, the amount, and when it applies, so nothing comes as a surprise.

Version 2026-11-08. These are the charges currently in force.

This is the schedule of charges referred to in our Terms of Service, our Risk Disclosures and the member app. The figures below are published from the same source the app charges from, so the price list you read here is the price you pay.

We do not apply charges we have not disclosed

If we introduce a new charge, or change an amount, we tell you in the app before it takes effect and we update this page. A charge that is not on this page is not applied to your account.

Clarity by omission

What we do not charge

A price list is only complete if it also says what is absent. These charges do not apply to a Coopvest Africa member account.

  • Withdrawal fee
  • Early-repayment penalty
  • Loan application or processing fee
  • Account-maintenance charge beyond the monthly administrative fee

How this page stays accurate

The figures above are generated from the cooperative's own fee data and published to this page automatically. They are not retyped by hand, because a figure written down twice eventually disagrees with itself, and a price list that disagrees with the price charged is worse than no price list at all.

Your own obligations are always visible in the member app, under Settings → About → Fees & Charges, together with any amount outstanding on your account.
