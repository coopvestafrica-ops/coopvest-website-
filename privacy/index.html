Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, share and protect personal data.

Draft for legal review — last updated 2026. Have this reviewed by qualified Nigerian counsel before launch.

This policy explains how Coopvest Africa ("Coopvest", "we", "us") handles personal data when you visit this website, enquire about membership, join a scheme, or use our member services. It should be read with our Terms of Service and Cookie Policy.

1. Who we are

Coopvest Africa operates a cooperative savings and financing platform for salaried workers and partner institutions. For questions about this policy or your data, contact privacy@coopvest.africa.

2. Data we collect

  • Contact and enquiry data. Name, email address, phone number and the content of your message when you contact us.
  • Membership and identity data. Information provided during registration and identity verification (KYC), such as your name, date of birth, contact details and identity document information, to the extent required by applicable law.
  • Financial data. Contributions, loan balances, repayments, fees and transaction records associated with your membership.
  • Employment and payroll data. Where you participate through an employer scheme, the deduction details your employer remits on your behalf.
  • Technical data. IP address, device and browser information, and session data generated when you use our services.

3. How we use your data

  • To assess and administer your membership, contributions and any loan.
  • To meet identity verification and anti-fraud obligations.
  • To communicate with you about your account, and to respond to your enquiries.
  • To comply with legal, regulatory and audit requirements.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of our platform.

4. Lawful basis for processing

We process personal data where it is necessary to perform our agreement with you, to comply with legal obligations, to pursue legitimate interests such as fraud prevention and platform security, or on the basis of your consent where consent is the appropriate basis.

5. Sharing your data

We do not sell personal data. We may share it with:

  • Your employer or institution, only to the extent needed to operate the deduction scheme, and not for their independent marketing.
  • Service providers who support our platform (hosting, identity verification, communications, payment processing), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Regulators and law enforcement, where we are legally required to disclose.

6. Data retention

We retain personal data for as long as your membership is active and thereafter for the period required to meet legal, tax, accounting and regulatory obligations. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

7. Security

We protect personal data with encryption in transit, role-based access controls, identity verification before financial activity, and audit logging of operational actions. No system is perfectly secure, but we design so that a single failure does not expose everything.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal data, correction of inaccuracies, deletion where there is no overriding obligation to retain it, restriction of processing, and a portable copy of data you provided. To exercise a right, contact privacy@coopvest.africa.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

This website uses a minimal set of cookies and local storage. Details are set out in our Cookie Policy.

10. Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be communicated through the platform or by email, and the "last updated" date above will change.

12. Contact

Questions, concerns or complaints about privacy can be sent to privacy@coopvest.africa or through our contact page.
