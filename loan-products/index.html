How the interest is calculated

Interest is a flat percentage of the amount you borrow over the full tenure:

Total repayment = amount + (amount × rate)
Monthly repayment = total repayment ÷ tenure

A ₦100,000 Stable Loan over 12 months at 5% therefore costs ₦105,000 in total — ₦8,750 a month. There are no compounding charges and no early-settlement surprises; the figure you are quoted is the figure you repay.

Paying less interest

  • Referrals. Confirmed referrals earn an interest discount of up to 2%, taking the rate down from your product's base rate.
  • Choose the lowest rate for the job. If you can manage the larger instalment, the Stable Loan (12 months) at 5% is the cheapest product we offer.
  • Borrow only what you need. Interest is charged on the amount borrowed, so a smaller loan costs less.

A discount never takes a rate below half of the product's base rate. See Loans for eligibility and repayment terms.

Requirements

What every application needs

Verified membership

Identity verification (KYC) must be complete, and your membership active, before any loan can be approved.

Three guarantors

Each loan requires 3 guarantors. You generate a QR code in the app and each guarantor accepts it in turn.

Savings history

Your limit is a multiple of what you have saved. Consistent contributions raise both your limit and your standing.

Before you borrow

A loan is a commitment for its full tenure. If you have a loan that is overdue, defaulted or in recovery, you cannot take another until it is resolved. Please read the risk and financial disclosures first.
