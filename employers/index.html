For Employers & Institutions

An employee financial wellbeing benefit that runs through the payroll you already operate.

The partnership model

How the employer partnership works

Coopvest Africa partners with employers, cooperatives and institutions to deliver a savings and financing benefit to their staff. You authorise a monthly deduction from participating employees' salaries; we handle onboarding, verification, member records and servicing.

No new software for your team to learn. No additional reconciliation for your finance department beyond the remittance record we provide each cycle.

At a glance

  • Your input — one payroll deduction line.
  • Your systems — no change required.
  • Your records — per-cycle remittance report.
  • Your staff get — savings and credit access.
Salary deduction

Salary deduction authorisation

Every deduction is authorised by the employee individually. You are not asked to guess who has consented.

The employee authorises

Each participating employee signs a deduction authorisation stating the amount or percentage and the scheme. This is the legal basis for the deduction.

You deduct during payroll

The authorised amount is deducted as a payroll line item for participating employees only, and stated on the payslip.

You remit for the cycle

Deductions for the cycle are remitted to Coopvest Africa together, with the member-level breakdown we provide.

We reconcile and record

We match each remitted line to the correct member account, credit it, and confirm remittance back to you. Discrepancies are raised, not absorbed.

Benefits to employees

  • A consistent savings habit they do not have to remember to keep.
  • Access to affordable financing, earned through saving rather than collateral.
  • Clear records and statements they can rely on.
  • Reduced reliance on predatory lending for emergencies.

Benefits to institutions

  • Financial wellbeing for staff, at no cost to build or operate.
  • Fewer ad-hoc salary advance and emergency loan requests.
  • Auditable, per-cycle remittance records for finance and audit.
  • A concrete, visible staff benefit that supports retention.

Questions from institutions

Employer FAQs

Do we need to integrate with Coopvest technically?

No. Participation is expressed as a payroll deduction line. The only information exchanged each cycle is the deduction list and its remittance — no system integration, no software to install.

What are our responsibilities each cycle?

Deduct the authorised amounts for participating employees, remit them for the cycle, and share the deduction breakdown so we can credit the correct member accounts. We provide the template and reconcile the result.

What if an employee leaves or stops participating?

Deduction simply stops. They can continue as a direct member and keep contributing; if they exit, their position is settled under the scheme rules in force at the time. We notify you of any change that affects their deduction.

Are we liable for employee loans?

No. Loans are between the member and Coopvest Africa. Your role is limited to the authorised salary deduction. Guarantor arrangements, where a scheme requires them, are between members.

How is employee data protected?

Employee data is handled for the purpose of administering the scheme, protected in transit, and access is restricted by role within our operations. See our Privacy Policy for detail.
