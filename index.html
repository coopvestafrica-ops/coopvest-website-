The problem we solve

Saving is hard when nothing is structured.

Income arrives and quietly disappears. Emergency borrowing is expensive, informal savings are untracked, and fair credit is out of reach.

Coopvest Africa replaces that with a cooperative model: a contribution you choose, a visible record, and financing based on what you have saved.

  • Contribute an amount you choose, on a schedule that fits.
  • Build eligibility for financing as your savings grow.
  • See contributions, statements and balances in one place.

How it works

Four steps from sign-up to savings

A straightforward path, and two ways to contribute.

Join through your employer, or directly

If your employer partners with us, you are onboarded during a payroll cycle. If not, register directly.

Verify your identity

A short KYC step ties your account and records to a verified person.

Contribute on schedule

Contributions are deducted from salary or paid directly, and recorded immediately.

Access financing as you grow

Your savings history builds eligibility. Apply, get a repayment plan, track the balance to zero.

Pay through your employer, or pay yourself

Both routes build the same savings record and the same route to financing. Switch between them any time under Membership in the app.

Salary Deduction

Your employer deducts your contribution from salary each month and remits it for you. Nothing to remember, nothing to miss.

  • Best for: employees of a partner employer or cooperative.
  • How you pay: one payroll deduction line, authorised by you.
  • You need: employment details at registration.

Direct Deposit

You make the payment yourself by bank transfer, card or USSD, whenever it suits you. Full control over when and how much.

  • Best for: anyone not on a partner payroll, and the self-employed.
  • How you pay: bank transfer, card or USSD, at your own pace.
  • You need: just a verified email and phone number.

Questions

Answers to the things people ask first

Who can join Coopvest Africa?

Anyone, by either of two routes. Salary Deduction is for employees of a partner employer: your contribution comes out of salary and your employer remits it. Direct Deposit is for everyone else: pay by bank transfer, card or USSD, with no employment details needed.

How much can I contribute?

An amount you choose within your scheme's bands. Consistency is the point: a modest amount you maintain every month beats an ambitious one you stop paying.

When can I access a loan?

After you have completed identity verification and built a contribution history. Loan limits are based on your savings and repayment capacity, and are confirmed at application. See Loans for the detail.
