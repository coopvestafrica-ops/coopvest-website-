How this is calculated

Interest is a flat percentage of the amount you borrow over the full tenure, not compounding: total repayment = amount + (amount × rate), and monthly repayment = total repayment ÷ tenure. The same method is used on the loan products page, where every rate and worked example is set out.

Verified membership

Identity verification (KYC) must be complete, and your membership active, before any loan can be approved.

Three guarantors

Each loan requires 3 guarantors. You generate a QR code in the app and each guarantor accepts it in turn.

Savings history

A minimum of six months of consistent contributions, with identity verification complete, before you can apply.
