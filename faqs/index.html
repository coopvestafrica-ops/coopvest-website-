Frequently Asked Questions

The questions members and employers ask most, answered directly.

Membership

Joining and contributing

Who can join Coopvest Africa?

Anyone can join, by either of two routes. Salary Deduction is for employees whose employer partners with us: your contribution comes out of your salary each month and your employer remits it. Direct Deposit is for everyone else, and for anyone who would rather pay themselves: you make the payment by bank transfer, card or USSD, and no employment details are needed.

How much can I contribute?

You contribute an amount you choose within the bands agreed for your scheme, so that the commitment is affordable and sustainable.

Consistency matters more than size: a modest amount maintained every month builds far more than an ambitious one you stop paying after two cycles.

When can I access a loan?

After you have completed identity verification and built a contribution history of the minimum duration set by your scheme. Loan limits are based on your savings and repayment capacity, and are confirmed at application. There is no automatic entitlement. Each application is assessed.

How does salary deduction work?

You authorise your employer to deduct your agreed contribution from salary each cycle. Your employer remits the deduction and your member record is credited automatically, with a date and reference you can review. Deduction always rests on your individual written authorisation.

Can I contribute directly instead?

Yes. If you have no partner employer, or you leave one, you can continue as a direct member and contribute by bank transfer or a supported payment channel. Your savings history and eligibility continue to build exactly as before.

What happens if I leave my employer?

Salary deduction stops, but your membership and your records do not disappear. You can continue as a direct member and keep contributing. If you choose to exit the cooperative, your position is settled under the scheme rules in force at the time.

How are transactions tracked?

Every contribution, loan repayment, fee and adjustment is recorded against your account with a date, an amount and a reference. You can see the full history in your member account, and request a statement whenever you need one.

Is my money safe?

We apply identity verification before any account becomes financially active, encrypt data in transit, restrict internal access by role, and log operational actions for audit.

It is important to be clear, though: Coopvest is a cooperative savings and financing platform, not a bank. Please read our risk and financial disclosures, which set out the risks you should understand before joining.

What fees will I pay?

Any applicable fees, charges and penalties are disclosed to you at onboarding and in the schedule of charges, and are shown before you commit to a product. We do not rely on charges you discover later.

How do I get help with my account?

Contact us using the details on the Contact page. Questions about your balance, a contribution entry or an active loan are answered by our support team.

Borrowing

Loans and repayment

Do I need collateral to get a loan?

No. Eligibility is built from your savings and contribution record rather than pledged assets. Every loan does require 3 guarantors, who accept responsibility for the balance if you default.

How is my loan limit calculated?

It is a multiple of your savings balance: 3× for most products, 4× for the Premium Loan and 5× for the Maxi Loan, subject to your repayment capacity. The exact limit is confirmed in your loan offer.

What interest rate will I pay?

Rates depend on the product: 5.0% for the Stable Loan (12 months), 7.0% for the Flexi Loan and Stable Loan (18 months), 7.5% for the Quick Loan, 14.0% for the Premium Loan and 19.0% for the Maxi Loan.

Interest is a flat charge on the amount you borrow over the whole tenure, not compounding, and it is stated in your offer together with the monthly repayment and the total repayment, so you can see the true cost before you accept. Full detail is on the Loan Products & Rates page.

How much can I borrow?

Your limit is a multiple of what you have saved: 3× for the Quick, Flexi and both Stable Loans, 4× for the Premium Loan and 5× for the Maxi Loan. The minimum is ₦1,000.

So a member with ₦50,000 saved could borrow up to ₦150,000 on a Stable Loan, or ₦250,000 on a Maxi Loan. Your exact limit is confirmed when you apply.

Do I need guarantors?

Yes. Every loan requires 3 guarantors. You generate a QR code in the app and each guarantor accepts it in turn. Only guarantee a loan you would be willing and able to repay yourself.

Can I repay early?

Early repayment terms depend on your scheme and loan agreement. Ask us before making an early repayment so we can confirm how it will be applied to your balance.

What happens if I miss a repayment?

A missed or late repayment may attract a penalty and affect your future eligibility. Contact us before the due date if you anticipate difficulty. Options available beforehand are usually better than those available afterwards.

What does being a guarantor mean?

It means you accept responsibility for the outstanding balance if the borrower defaults. It is a serious commitment. Only guarantee a loan you would be willing and able to repay.

More on categories, tenures and guarantors is on the Loans page.

Institutions

Employer questions

How do we set up a scheme for our staff?

We agree eligibility, contribution bands and the deduction cycle with you, configure the scheme, then support your staff through enrolment. See For Employers for the full model.

Does participating require changes to our payroll system?

No. Participation is a payroll deduction line item. No integration, no software to install, and no change to how you process payroll.

Are we responsible for employee loans?

No. Loans are between the member and Coopvest Africa. Your role is limited to the authorised salary deduction.
