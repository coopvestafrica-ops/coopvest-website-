About Coopvest Africa

We exist to make structured saving and fair financing ordinary for salaried workers.

Our story

Built from a simple observation

Salaried workers in Nigeria earn predictably, yet very few build lasting financial security from it. Contributions to informal cooperatives are recorded by hand, statements rarely exist, and access to affordable credit depends on who you know rather than what you have saved.

Coopvest Africa was founded to close that gap. We pair the trusted, community-rooted model of a cooperative with the record-keeping, identity verification and transparency of modern financial technology — so members can see exactly where they stand, and institutions can run an employee benefit without adding administrative burden.

What we are building

  • A savings habit that survives the month it starts.
  • Financing priced against savings behaviour, not connections.
  • Financial records members actually own and can rely on.
  • A partnership model employers can adopt without new systems.

Our mission

To help salaried workers build durable financial security through consistent, transparent and affordable cooperative saving.

Our vision

A Nigeria where every salaried worker has a trustworthy, digital route to savings, credit and financial records — regardless of employer or income level.

Core values

The principles we hold ourselves to

Transparency

Every balance, fee and rate is visible to the member it concerns. We would rather explain a charge than bury it.

Stewardship

Member contributions are not our money. We treat every naira as held in trust, and we account for it accordingly.

Inclusion

Financial products should not require pre-existing wealth. Our model is designed for the member who is starting out.

Consistency

We reward the discipline of saving regularly, because that discipline is what actually builds wealth over time.

Our approach to financial inclusion

Inclusion is not a slogan; it is a design constraint. It shapes three decisions:

  • Affordability first. Contribution bands are set so a member can realistically keep paying.
  • Payroll as the rail. Where an employer is present, deduction removes the discipline problem entirely.
  • Credit follows savings. Eligibility is earned through participation, not collateral.
